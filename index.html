The Battle Rhythm Planner

Military commanders don’t start their day scattered. They run a battle rhythm — a structured daily brief that organizes priorities, people, and decisions before the day begins. This one-page planner brings that discipline to your workday.

  • Daily mission statement — your single focus for the day
  • Top 3 priority tasks, ranked by impact
  • People check-in — who needs your attention today
  • Decision log — track what you decided and why
  • End-of-day after-action review — 3 questions, 5 minutes

### Ohio licensing (Ohio State Cosmetology and Barber Board)
- **Contact:** ohiocos@cos.ohio.gov · (614) 466-3834 · cos.ohio.gov · exam center in **Dublin**
- **Natural Hair Stylist license:** 450 hours. Covers twisting, wrapping, weaving, extending, **locking**, braiding. **Non-chemical**; secondary sources also say **no cutting** — *unconfirmed, flagged*.
- **Boutique Service Registration (BTQR):** shorter braiding route, requires notarized trainer affidavit. **Unclear whether it covers locking** — flagged.
- **Cosmetologist:** 1,500 hours. Required for chemical services (loc coloring).
- **Independent Contractor license:** separately required for an independent working inside a licensed salon (Ohio Admin Code 4713-13-02). **~$75 issue / $70 renew.** Commonly missed by suite renters — directly applies to Robbie.
- **Salon license:** issued by location. **Open question:** does Sola hold one for the building, or does each suite need its own?
- **⚠ Sharp edge — ORC §4713.41** describes a salon license being issued on affirmation that **a Cosmetologist** has charge and immediate supervision of the salon. If literal, a Natural Hair Stylist may not be able to hold her own suite salon license. **Needs Board confirmation.**
- **⚠ Experience credit — Ohio Admin Code 4713-7-09:** a year of licensed experience may substitute for **100 training hours, up to 500**. Robbie's 6 years would cap at 500 vs the 450 required. **BUT** the provision sits in the *out-of-state license holder* rule and says *licensed* experience — so it may not apply to unlicensed Ohio practice. **Highest-value unknown: worth $7,500 and a season of her life.**
- **Instructor license:** requires an *advanced* license in the branch. Natural Hair Stylist Instructors teach braiding/artificial hair in Ohio-licensed schools.
- **CE:** required every two years for renewal.
- **Inspections:** random within 90 days of a new license; licensed facilities at least every two years.

**Board fees (secondary sources — Ohio revised fee rules eff. Jan 2, 2025):** NHS exam $40 + initial license $45 = **$85**; Independent Contractor $75 issue / $70 renew; cosmetology/barbering renewal $65; temporary pre-exam work permit $15; re-exam $35. **Salon license fee unknown.**

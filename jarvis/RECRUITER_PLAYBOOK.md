## 📧 TIER 3: PERSONALIZED OUTREACH

### Method 1: LinkedIn Message (HIGHEST CONVERSION)

**Subject:** Reference their company + specific program

**Template:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] is expanding its [specific program area]. 
As someone with 25+ years leading $30M+ federal programs and managing 
large training teams, I thought we should connect.

My background:
- [Specific achievement that matches their initiative]
- [Relevant credential or clearance]
- [Relevant experience]

Would be great to grab coffee/call and discuss how I might add value 
to your organization's [their focus area].

Best,
Kenyatta
kenyattawilliams1121@gmail.com
937-477-8210
```

**Rules:**
- ✅ Mention their company by name
- ✅ Reference a specific program/initiative they're doing
- ✅ Keep to 3-4 sentences max
- ✅ Include credentials (25 years, clearance, budget experience)
- ✅ Add your email + phone (they can reach out)
- ❌ Don't ask for a job
- ❌ Don't ask to "connect"
- ❌ Don't be generic ("great opportunities")

### Method 2: Direct Email

**Subject Line:**
```
Director-Level Program Manager + [Company] + Your Specific Initiative
Example: "Director-Level Program Manager | Booz Allen Training Delivery"
```

**Email Template:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I'm Kenyatta S. Williams, and I lead program management and training 
delivery for federal contracting organizations.

I saw [Company] is [specific initiative they're doing]. Given my 
background in this space, I wanted to reach out directly.

Quick background:

✓ Managed $30M training contracts with 20+ person teams
✓ Active Top Secret/SCI clearance
✓ Expertise in: program delivery, training design, workforce strategy

I'm not actively job hunting, but [Company] is exactly the type of 
organization I'd want to work with. Would love a 15-minute call to 
discuss how we might create value together.

Available [YOUR AVAILABILITY]

Best,
Kenyatta S. Williams
kenyattawilliams1121@gmail.com
937-477-8210
LinkedIn: [Your URL]
```

**Rules:**
- ✅ Specific about their initiative
- ✅ Position yourself as not desperately seeking job
- ✅ Emphasize VALUE you bring, not what you want
- ✅ Keep under 150 words
- ✅ Make it easy to respond (phone, email, clear availability)
- ❌ No attachments (unless asked)
- ❌ No generic "I'm looking for a job" tone

### Method 3: Direct Phone Call (If You Find Number)

**Script:**

## 📊 RELATIONSHIP BUILDING

- [ ] Visit their LinkedIn profile

- [ ] Light email: "Saw your company won [contract/award], congrats!"
- [ ] LinkedIn message: "Would love to catch up for coffee/call"
- [ ] No pressure, just staying top-of-mind

## 🎯 OPPORTUNITY SCORING

When you interact with a decision-maker, score the interaction:

**Score 3 = HOT LEAD** (Immediate pursuit)
- They replied positively
- They're hiring now (mentioned in message)
- They suggested meeting
- → Add to "Active Pursuit" list

**Score 2 = WARM LEAD** (Keep building relationship)
- They replied (even brief response)
- They're interested but not hiring yet
- They engaged with your content
- → Continue bi-weekly engagement

**Score 1 = COLD LEAD** (Keep on radar)
- No response yet
- Initial message delivered
- Will follow up in 3-4 weeks
- → Continue monthly touch-ins

## 📞 MONTHLY RECRUITER REVIEW

**Every 4 weeks, analyze:**
- How many contacts made?
- Response rate? (target 30-40%)
- How many meetings scheduled? (target 2-4)
- Best-performing outreach method?
- Which companies most responsive?
- Salary ranges discussed?

**Adjust strategy based on data:**
- If low response: Refine messaging
- If high response but wrong fit: Tighten targeting
- If getting interviews: Scale up outreach
- If no salary discussions: Add compensation to message
